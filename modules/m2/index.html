Hook

Picture this

The objective says “Understand the change-gate approval process.” The session is 45 minutes of slides.

Quick prompt (not scored): What would “application” look like here—and could one instructor actually see it with 124 people?

Teach — Align, then chunk for one-instructor runtime

Words like “understand” and “know”—call them awareness verbs—plus a lecture-only stack hide whether anyone can actually use the process. At 100+, one instructor has nothing observable to sample. Fix the objective first. Then cut the session into short pieces with a real practice beat in each.

Alignment chain (in this order)

  1. Measurable application objective — something you can see: complete a checklist, decide a fork, produce a one-page artifact. Not “recall the definition” alone.
  2. Activity practice moment — learners commit, decide, or make something you can sample.
  3. How you’ll know at scale — poll, template row, self-score sample, whole-room theme notes—not grading every essay tonight.

Chunking for one-instructor runtime

Keep each segment short enough to teach. Put one generative practice (learners produce or decide something new) in each chunk. Short segments with a clear learner action beat a long slide stack that ends with “any questions?”

Directions that work

Participant directions must state purpose, timing, roles/output, how 100+ people participate, and a help path—not “discuss, then we’ll share.”

Worked example — Objective + 3-segment outline

Strong — application objective

Given a short change-request scenario, the learner will complete a 5-step process checklist for one case and submit a one-page process artifact that a peer can score against a 4-bullet rubric.

Weak

“Understand the change-gate approval process” plus Lecture / Breakout / Any questions? Learners never practice the process, and the directions never say who does what—so both application demand and directions clarity fail.

Strong directions (Segment 2)

Purpose: Draft the missing process step for your role. Timing: 10 minutes. Roles: even roster number = Requestor; odd = Approver. Output: one completed template row (not a spoken report). How 100+ people participate: everyone types—we will not hear from every pair. Help: post in the Help thread with your roster number.

What “good enough” looks like

  • Objective states observable application performance
  • Each segment has a learner action · ≥1 generative practice moment
  • Chunks fit one-instructor runtime at 100+
  • Participant directions for ≥1 segment: purpose, timing, roles/output, participation-at-scale, help path
