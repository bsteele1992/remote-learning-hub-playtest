Facilitated remote at scale

You’re designing live remote sessions where one instructor faces about 124 learners—often with no teaching assistants. Seven short modules walk you through: spot why classroom plans break, write objectives people can actually do, pick activity patterns that scale, build checks for learning and for grading, redesign multi-role process walkthroughs, then ship a section-level package. You learn first. Then you practice.
